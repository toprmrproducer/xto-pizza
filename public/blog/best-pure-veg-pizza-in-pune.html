Best pure veg pizza in Pune, from Rs 99

Updated 19 October 2026 · XTO Pizza, Pune

Pune has no shortage of pizza places, but a fully vegetarian kitchen that does not charge a premium is harder to find. XTO Pizza is a pure veg pizzeria with two outlets on the Sinhagad Road side of the city: Shivane near Khadakwasla, and Nanded City. Between the two, Google shows 4.0 stars from more than 100 reviews.

Why pure veg matters here

Every pizza, burger, pasta and shake at XTO is vegetarian. There is no meat on the menu, so there is nothing to ask the staff about. For families and anyone who stays vegetarian, that makes ordering simple.

What you can order, and what it costs

Prices below are for the regular size on our live menu.

  • Margherita: Rs 99. Fresh tomato and cheddar. The honest test of any pizza place.
  • Paneer Makhani: Rs 149. Paneer, onion, capsicum and paprika in a makhni sauce.
  • Dil Desi: Rs 189. Capsicum, onion and tomato under a generous layer of cheese.
  • Farmer's Delight: Rs 209. Mushroom, tomato, capsicum, onion and sweet corn.
  • Tandoori Paneer Tikka: Rs 229. Paneer tikka, black olives, capsicum and fresh coriander.
  • Pesto Painting: Rs 259. Paneer, jalapeno and basil pesto.

If you want something other than pizza, the menu also has pasta (Arabiatta, Veg Alfredo, Tandoori), stacked burgers, wraps, loaded fries and thick shakes.

How to pick

First visit: start with the Margherita and one paneer pizza to share. If you like heat, go for Veggie Mexican with jalapeno. If you want something filling for the price, the combos start at Rs 159 and include a burger, fries and garlic bread.

Dine in or take away

Both outlets serve dine-in and takeaway. You can browse the menu and place an order from this website before you walk in.
