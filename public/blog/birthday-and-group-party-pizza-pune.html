Parties and groups

Birthday and group parties near Khadakwasla and Nanded City

Updated 19 October 2026 · XTO Pizza, Pune

Finding a place for a birthday, a college get-together or a family dinner on the Sinhagad Road side of Pune usually comes down to three questions: will everyone find something to eat, will the bill stay sensible, and can you actually book it. XTO Pizza is a pure veg pizza cafe with outlets at Shivane (Khadakwasla) and Nanded City, and it works well for groups that want to share.

Food that suits a mixed group

Because the whole kitchen is vegetarian, nobody at the table has to be catered around. Pizzas, pasta, burgers, wraps, loaded fries and nachos are all on one menu, so a group of eight can split into pizza people, pasta people and burger people without anyone being left out.

Party combos on the menu

  • Party Combo 1: Rs 449. A grilled sandwich, a regular premium pizza and fries.
  • Party Combo 2: Rs 899. Two medium pizzas with two coolers (Blue Lagoon or Summer Cooler).
  • Magic Combo: Rs 289. Two veg burgers, a 7 inch Margherita and fries.

For larger groups, order a mix of medium and large pizzas plus two or three sides to share, then add shakes. Thick shakes start at Rs 89 and the loaded XTO Special Shake is Rs 149.

How to book

For a birthday or any group booking, call the outlet you want to visit ahead of time so the team can plan seating. Tell them the number of people and the time. Table availability, decorations and any special arrangements are agreed directly with the outlet, so please confirm them on the call.

Which outlet

Choose Shivane if you are coming from Khadakwasla or NDA Road. Choose Nanded City if you are coming from Sinhagad Road or Nanded City itself.
