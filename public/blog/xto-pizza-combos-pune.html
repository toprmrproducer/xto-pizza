XTO Pizza combos in Pune: what is inside and the price

Updated 17 November 2026 · XTO Pizza, Pune

XTO Pizza is a pure veg pizza cafe in Pune, with outlets in Nanded City and Shivane (Khadakwasla). Here is exactly what is in each combo and what it costs.

Every combo and what is inside

Combo Crush - Rs 1591 veg burger, 1 french fries, 2 pcs garlic bread / any one 250ml (coke, thums up or sprite)
Magic Combo - Rs 2892 veg burgers, 1 margherita pizza 7 inch, 1 french fries
King Combo - Rs 329Any one 7 inch pizza (onion cheese, tomato cheese, margherita, sweet corn), 1 salsa nachos, any one 250ml drink
Combo Magambo - Rs 4292 veg burgers, any one 7 inch premium pizza, 2 pcs garlic bread or 1 french fries, any two 250ml drinks
Party Combo 1 - Rs 4491 veg grilled sandwich, 1 premium pizza (regular), 1 french fries, 2 frooti
Party Combo 2 - Rs 8992 medium pizzas (any), 2 blue lagoon / summer cooler

Which combo should you pick?

  • Solo and quick: Combo Crush, Rs 159, a burger with fries and garlic bread or a drink.
  • Two people: King Combo (Rs 329) or Combo Magambo (Rs 429).
  • Group or party: Party Combo 1 (Rs 449) or Party Combo 2 (Rs 899). See also our party pizza guide.

Delivery is free on orders of Rs 250 or more including GST, which every combo above Rs 159 clears. Details in the delivery guide.

Questions people ask

What is in the Combo Crush at XTO Pizza?

Combo Crush is Rs 159 and includes 1 veg burger, 1 french fries and 2 pieces of garlic bread, or one 250 ml drink (Coke, Thums Up or Sprite).

Which XTO Pizza combo is best for a group?

Party Combo 2 at Rs 899 has 2 medium pizzas of your choice and 2 mocktail coolers (blue lagoon or summer cooler). Combo Magambo at Rs 429 suits two people.

Are XTO Pizza combos vegetarian?

Yes. XTO Pizza is a pure veg kitchen, so every combo is vegetarian.
