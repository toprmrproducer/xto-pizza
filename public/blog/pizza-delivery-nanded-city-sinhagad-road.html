Pizza delivery in Nanded City and Sinhagad Road

Updated 17 November 2026 · XTO Pizza, Pune

XTO Pizza delivers pure veg pizza, burgers, pasta, wraps and shakes from its Nanded City outlet on Sinhagad Road. Order on xtopizza.in, pay online, and follow your rider on a live map.

How ordering works

  • Choose Delivery, share your location or type your address, and add items.
  • The outlet sees your order and confirms it before the kitchen starts. If it cannot, you are refunded automatically.
  • When the order is on its way you can watch the rider on a live map with distance and time left, and call them from the order page.

What to order for Rs 250 and free delivery

Pizzas start at Rs 99 for a Margherita, so most two-item orders cross the Rs 250 free-delivery line. The combos start at Rs 159 and go up to Rs 899 for a party of four.

Questions people ask

Does XTO Pizza deliver in Nanded City?

Yes. You can order delivery on xtopizza.in from the Nanded City outlet. Delivery covers addresses within 5 km of the outlet, measured in a straight line from the shop.

How much is the delivery charge?

Delivery is free when your order is Rs 250 or more including GST. Below that, delivery is Rs 50.

Can I track my delivery?

Yes. After the outlet accepts your order and a rider picks it up, the order page shows the rider's name, a live map, the distance and an estimated time, and a call link.

What if the outlet cannot make my order?

If the outlet rejects the order or does not confirm it in time, your online payment is refunded automatically in full.

Prefer to pick up?

Takeaway and dine-in are available at both outlets with the same menu and the same pure veg kitchen.
