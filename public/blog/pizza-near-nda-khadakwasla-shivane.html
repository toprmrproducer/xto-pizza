Pizza near NDA Road, Khadakwasla and Shivane

Updated 19 October 2026 · XTO Pizza, Pune

If you live, study or work around NDA Road, Khadakwasla, Shivane or Yashwant Nagar, XTO Pizza is the pure veg pizza cafe near Add Mart on NDA Rd. It has a 4.0 rating on Google from 78 reviews and serves dine-in and takeaway.

What people come for

  • Stone-baked veg pizzas, from a Rs 99 Margherita to a Rs 259 Pesto Painting.
  • Pasta in red, white and mixed sauces, with half portions from Rs 169.
  • Burgers from Rs 59 and wraps from Rs 139.
  • Thick shakes, coolers and cold coffee, from Rs 49.

Good for students and quick meals

The menu has plenty under Rs 150: a Tomato and Cheese 6 inch pizza at Rs 59, a Veg Burger at Rs 59, a Veg Grilled Sandwich at Rs 89 and French Fries at Rs 89. The Combo Crush at Rs 159 gives you a burger, fries and garlic bread.

Getting here

The outlet is on NDA Rd, near Add Mart, in Yashwant Nagar, Deshmukh Nagar, Shivane. It is a short drive from Khadakwasla and the NDA gate. Open the Google Maps link on our contact page for directions.
