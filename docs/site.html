Our Story

Born on Sinhagad Road

XTO Pizza started with one oven, one recipe book and a simple belief: Pune deserves a proper local pizzeria without the franchise price tag. Every dough is stretched by hand, every sauce is made in-house, and nothing leaves the counter unless we would eat it ourselves.

Today two outlets serve Khadakwasla, Shivane and Nanded City with the same promise: pure veg, generous cheese, honest prices.
